    Populations

    Your browser blocked automatic copying. The table is selected below: press Ctrl+C (or ⌘C) to copy it.

    Gates apply to every loaded sample, matched by channel name, so you can switch tubes and compare. Right-click an axis title (or click it with the Select tool) to change its channel or scale, or to rename the channel. Right-click inside a plot to copy it for Word, PowerPoint or Excel. Click a gate to select it. Drag inside it to move it, or drag its handles to resize it: corners and sides for rectangles, corners for polygons, ends for histogram ranges. Double-click a polygon's edge to add a corner, or a corner to remove it. A quadrant moves as one: drag its centre, either line, or anywhere inside it. Press Esc during a drag to undo it, or Delete to remove the selected gate. To draw a polygon, click to add corners, then double-click or click the first corner to close.

    Compensation

    Off

    Spillover matrix

    Each row is one dye's detector. The numbers are how much of that signal, as a percentage, also lands in the other detectors. Type to edit; plots update as you go.

    Channels to compensate

    Calculate from single-stain controls

    Load an unstained tube and one tube per dye stained with that dye alone. Auto-compensate finds the control tubes, picks out the cells and calculates the matrix in one step. To do it by hand, assign the tubes below and press Calculate.

    While compensation is on, plots, gates and statistics use compensated values, and those axes are labelled Comp-. Compensation can push events below zero: switch fluorescence axes to Biexponential to see them instead of piling up on the log axis.

    Cell cycle

    Auto-gating first keeps single cells: clumps of two cells have twice the DNA area but not twice the height. It then fits the DNA histogram with a G0/G1 peak, a G2/M peak at about twice the DNA, and S phase between them. It adds gates and two plots so you can check the result. To try it, pick Example: cell cycle, untreated or G2/M arrest in the sample list.

    Channel names

    Your labels appear on axes, menus, tables and exports. The channel itself (FL1-A) stays the same, so gates still match across files. You can also right-click an axis title and choose Rename.
